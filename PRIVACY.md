# Duely Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 20, 2026_

Duely helps you keep track of your subscriptions, their cost and their renewal dates. It's built so that your information stays on your device. Duely has no user accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics and no advertising.

This policy also appears inside the app under **Settings › Privacy**. If the two ever differ, this document is the reference.

## Information stored on your device

Duely saves the following, only in the app's storage on your iPhone or iPad:

- the name you enter during onboarding
- your monthly budget and currency
- the subscriptions you add (name, price, billing cycle, category, color, payment date, reminder choice)
- your weekly check-in answers (whether you used each subscription in a given week)

This information isn't sent to us or to anyone else. Like other app data, it's included in your device's iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on, under the protections Apple provides for those backups.

## Notifications

Check-in and payment reminders are scheduled with local notifications on your device. Duely doesn't use a push notification service, so no reminder content leaves your device.

## Import from Email (Gmail, optional)

You can choose to connect a Gmail account so Duely can find subscriptions in your receipts and renewal emails. This feature is off until you turn it on.

**What Duely accesses.** When you connect, you sign in on Google's own page (Duely never sees your password) and Google asks you to allow one permission: `gmail.readonly`, read-only access to your messages. Duely uses it to:

- read your Gmail address, to show which account is connected;
- search for recent emails that look like receipts, renewals or plan confirmations;
- download those emails, up to 60 per scan.

Duely can't send, delete, label or change any email.

**How it's processed.** Emails are downloaded directly from Google to your device over an encrypted connection. They're read by Apple Intelligence using the language model that runs **on your device** (Apple's Foundation Models framework, on-device model only). Email content is:

- never sent to Duely's developers, to Apple, or to any other third party;
- never used to train or improve any AI model;
- held in memory only while a scan runs, one email at a time, and never saved to storage or logs.

**What's kept.** The scan produces a list of suggested subscriptions (service name, price, currency, billing cycle, category, payment date). Nothing is added until you review the list and tap Add. Only the subscriptions you choose are saved, in the same way as subscriptions you enter by hand.

**Your sign-in.** The access and refresh tokens Google issues are stored in your device's Keychain, available only on that device and not synced through iCloud Keychain.

**Disconnecting.** In **Settings › Import from Email › Disconnect Gmail**, Duely revokes its access with Google and deletes the tokens from your device. You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account at <https://myaccount.google.com/connections>. Subscriptions you already added stay in the app until you delete them.

### Google API Services User Data Policy

Duely's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

- Gmail data is used only to provide the user-facing feature of finding subscriptions in your email;
- Gmail data is not transferred to anyone, except as necessary to provide that feature, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets with notice to you. In practice, it never leaves your device;
- Gmail data is not used for advertising, including retargeting, personalized or interest-based advertising;
- no human reads your Gmail data. Duely's developers have no way to access it.

## Apple Intelligence

Email analysis uses only Apple's on-device model and requires a device that supports Apple Intelligence with Apple Intelligence turned on. Duely doesn't use Private Cloud Compute or any server-based model for this feature.

## Tracking and third parties

Duely doesn't track you across apps or websites, doesn't include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and doesn't sell or share data with data brokers. The only external service Duely contacts is Google, and only if you connect Gmail.

## Children

Duely isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

## Deleting your data

- Delete individual subscriptions in the app.
- Disconnect Gmail in Settings to revoke access and delete the sign-in.
- Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device.

Because Duely doesn't hold your data on any server, there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here and in the app, with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes to how email is handled will be explained in the app before they take effect.
