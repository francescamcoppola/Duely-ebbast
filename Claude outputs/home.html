What MyDues does

MyDues is an iPhone app that helps you keep track of your subscriptions, recurring payments, purchases and warranties: what each one costs and when the next payment, return deadline or warranty expiry is due. It reminds you before a deadline so you are never caught out, and it shows how your payments add up against the monthly budget you set.

  • Add subscriptions, purchases and warranties in a few taps, or let the app suggest them from your emails.
  • See upcoming payments and your total spending by month.
  • Get a reminder before each payment, renewal or deadline.
  • MyDues has no user accounts of its own, no servers, no analytics and no advertising. Your data stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud.

Optional: import from Gmail

If you choose, you can connect your Gmail account so MyDues can find subscriptions, orders and warranties in your emails instead of you typing them in. This feature is off until you turn it on in Settings › Import from Email.

How it works

  • You sign in on Google's own page and approve a single permission: read-only access to your Gmail (gmail.readonly). MyDues can never send, delete or change an email.
  • MyDues searches for recent receipts, invoices, renewal notices, order confirmations and warranty documents, and reads them one at a time.
  • Each email is analysed by Apple Intelligence, on your device. The text of your emails is not sent to Apple, Google or anyone else for this step, and MyDues does not use any cloud model for it.
  • MyDues shows you what it found. For a subscription: the service name, price, billing frequency and payment date. For an order or warranty: the shop or product, price, purchase date and the return or warranty deadline. Nothing is added until you review the list and tap Add.

What is kept and what is not

  • Email content is held in memory only while a scan is running. It is not saved to disk, logged, or transmitted to any server.
  • The only thing saved is the subscriptions, orders and warranties you choose to add. They are stored like anything else you enter in the app: on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your private iCloud, which only you can access.
  • Your Google sign-in credentials are stored in your device's Keychain, only on that device, and are not synced to iCloud.
  • There is no MyDues server in this process: your emails go from Google directly to your phone.
  • You can disconnect Gmail at any time in Settings, which also revokes the access you gave. You can also remove access from your Google Account permissions page.

The scan requires an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on.

Google user data

MyDues's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

MyDues uses Gmail data only to detect and show you the subscriptions, orders and warranties in your own emails. It is not used for advertising, is not sold or shared with third parties, and no person reads your emails. For the full details, see our Privacy Policy.
