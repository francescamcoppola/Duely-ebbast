What Duely does

Duely is an iPhone app that helps you keep track of your subscriptions: what each one costs, how often it is charged and when the next payment is due. It reminds you before a renewal so you are never surprised by a charge, and it shows how your subscriptions add up against the monthly budget you set.

  • Add subscriptions in a few taps, or let the app suggest them from your receipts.
  • See upcoming payments and your total spending by month.
  • Get a reminder before each renewal.
  • Your data stays on your device. Duely has no user accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics and no advertising.

Optional: import from Gmail

If you choose, you can connect your Gmail account so Duely can find subscriptions in your billing emails instead of you typing them in. This feature is off until you turn it on in Settings › Import from Email.

How it works

  • You sign in on Google's own page and approve a single permission: read-only access to your Gmail (gmail.readonly). Duely can never send, delete or change an email.
  • Duely searches for recent receipts, renewal notices and subscription confirmations, and reads them one at a time.
  • Each email is analysed by Apple Intelligence, on your device. The text of your emails is not sent to Apple, Google or anyone else for this step, and Duely does not use any cloud model for it.
  • Duely shows you the subscriptions it found, such as the service name, price, billing frequency and payment date. Nothing is added until you review the list and tap Add.

What is kept and what is not

  • Email content is held in memory only while a scan is running. It is not saved to disk, logged, or transmitted to any server.
  • The only thing saved is the subscriptions you choose to add, stored on your device like any other subscription in the app.
  • Your Google sign-in credentials are stored in your device's Keychain, only on that device, and are not synced to iCloud.
  • There is no Duely server in this process: your emails go from Google directly to your phone.
  • You can disconnect Gmail at any time in Settings, which also revokes the access you gave. You can also remove access from your Google Account permissions page.

The scan requires an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on.

Google user data

Duely's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Duely uses Gmail data only to detect and show you the subscriptions in your own emails. It is not used for advertising, is not sold or shared with third parties, and no person at Duely reads your emails. For the full details, see our Privacy Policy.
