Gmail import (optional)

MyDues can optionally connect to your Gmail account to find your subscriptions, orders and warranties. This feature is off unless you turn it on in Settings › Import from Email.

What MyDues accesses. If you connect Gmail, you grant MyDues read-only access to your mailbox (the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly permission). MyDues searches for recent receipts, invoices, renewal notices, order confirmations and warranty documents and reads those emails one at a time. It cannot send, delete or modify any email.

How the data is used. The text of each email is analysed by Apple Intelligence, using the model that runs on your device. It is not sent to Apple, Google or anyone else for this step, and Private Cloud Compute and other server-based models are not used. MyDues uses this information only to show you what it found so you can review it: for a subscription, the service name, price, billing frequency and payment date; for an order or warranty, the shop or product, price, purchase date and the return or warranty deadline. Nothing is added until you confirm.

What is stored. Email content is kept in memory only while a scan is running. It is not saved to disk, logged, or sent to any server, and MyDues has no servers that receive it. The only thing saved is the subscriptions, orders and warranties you choose to add, stored like anything else you enter in the app: on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your private iCloud database (see "iCloud sync"). Your Google sign-in credentials (access and refresh tokens) are stored in your device's Keychain, only on that device, and are not synced to iCloud.

Sharing. MyDues does not sell, share or transfer your Gmail data to third parties, does not use it for advertising, and no person at MyDues reads your emails.

Limited Use. MyDues's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and deleting. You can disconnect Gmail at any time in Settings › Import from Email, which revokes MyDues's access and removes the stored sign-in credentials from your device. You can also remove access from your Google Account permissions page. Items you added remain in the app until you delete them; deleting them, or deleting the app, removes them from your device, and from your private iCloud if you use iCloud sync.
