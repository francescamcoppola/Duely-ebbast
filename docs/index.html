Frequently asked questions

What is MyDues?

MyDues keeps track of your subscriptions and other recurring payments (streaming, bills, taxes and fees, tuition, insurance), their cost and their due dates, and reminds you before they renew.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone or iPad running iOS 27. Importing from email also needs a device with Apple Intelligence turned on.

Where is my data stored? Do I need an account?

There are no accounts and no servers of ours. Your data lives on your device and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud so it syncs across your devices and survives a reinstall. We can't see it. Details in the Privacy Policy.

My reminders don't arrive.
  1. Open Settings › Notifications › MyDues and make sure notifications are allowed.
  2. Check that the subscription has a reminder set in its detail screen.
  3. Check that no Focus mode is silencing the app.
Data doesn't sync between my devices.

Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices, make sure iCloud is on for MyDues (Settings › your name › iCloud), and keep both devices online for a few minutes. Without iCloud, data stays on the single device.

How do I delete my data?
  • Delete single subscriptions in the app; the deletion syncs to iCloud.
  • Settings › Reset MyDues erases everything from this device, your iCloud and your other devices, and disconnects every Gmail account.
  • Deleting the app removes the data on that device only; iCloud data stays until you reset the app.
The app suggested a wrong subscription or price.

Suggestions come from Apple Intelligence reading your emails, and it can make mistakes. Nothing is added until you review the list and tap Add, and you can edit any subscription afterwards.

What is Screen Time tracking?

Optional. You can link a subscription to the app you use it in, and MyDues pre-fills your weekly check-in and reminds you if a paid app hasn't been opened for a week. The app never learns which app you picked or what you did in it. It may not be available in every build or on the Simulator.

Import from Email (Gmail)

What does the Gmail import do?

It finds subscriptions and recurring payments in your receipts, renewals, bills and payment notices from the last 13 months. You can connect up to five Gmail accounts. It is off until you turn it on in Settings › Import from Email.

What does MyDues read? Is my email safe?

Google asks you to allow one permission, gmail.readonly: read-only. MyDues can't send, delete, label or change any email. Emails go straight from Google to your device and are read by Apple Intelligence's on-device model: they are never sent to us, to Apple or to anyone else, never used to train AI, and never saved to storage. See the Privacy Policy.

The scan says Apple Intelligence is not available or still getting ready.
  • Use a device that supports Apple Intelligence and turn it on in Settings (search “Apple Intelligence” in Settings if you can't find it).
  • Keep the device on Wi‑Fi and charging while the model downloads; it needs several GB of free space.
  • The device language and the Siri language must be the same supported language.
  • Check Settings › Screen Time › Content & Privacy Restrictions and any management profile (Settings › General › VPN & Device Management) for blocks.
  • Restart the device. The Scan button enables itself as soon as the model is ready.
Google shows “Google hasn't verified this app” or sign-in fails.

While the Gmail import is being rolled out, sign-in may be limited to accounts we've added as testers, and Google shows this warning. If you haven't been invited, write to us. Personal @gmail.com accounts work best; work or school accounts may be blocked by your administrator.

I have to reconnect Gmail.

Sign-in tokens are kept only in your device's Keychain and aren't synced, so on a new device, after a reset, or if Google expires the access, connect the account again in Settings › Import from Email.

How do I disconnect Gmail?

In Settings › Import from Email, swipe the account and tap Disconnect: MyDues revokes its access and deletes the tokens from your device. You can also remove access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/connections. Subscriptions you already added stay until you delete them.
