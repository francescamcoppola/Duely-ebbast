Frequently asked questions

What is MyDues?

MyDues keeps track of your subscriptions and other recurring payments (streaming, bills, taxes and fees, tuition, insurance), their cost and their due dates, and reminds you before they renew.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone or iPad running iOS 26.6 or later. Some suggestions (finding a service's website, plans and cancellation page) use Apple Intelligence when the device supports it.

Where is my data stored? Do I need an account?

There are no accounts and no servers of ours. Your data lives on your device and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud so it syncs across your devices and survives a reinstall. We can't see it. Details in the Privacy Policy.

My reminders don't arrive.
  1. Open Settings › Notifications › MyDues and make sure notifications are allowed.
  2. Check that the subscription has a reminder set in its detail screen.
  3. Check that no Focus mode is silencing the app.
Data doesn't sync between my devices.

Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices, make sure iCloud is on for MyDues (Settings › your name › iCloud), and keep both devices online for a few minutes. Without iCloud, data stays on the single device.

How do I delete my data?
  • Delete single subscriptions in the app; the deletion syncs to iCloud.
  • Settings › Reset MyDues erases everything from this device, your iCloud and your other devices.
  • Deleting the app removes the data on that device only; iCloud data stays until you reset the app.
A suggested plan, price or link is wrong.

Plans and typical prices come from a list in the app and may be out of date, and links found with Apple Intelligence can be wrong. You can edit any subscription at any time; if a well-known service has wrong data, write to us.
