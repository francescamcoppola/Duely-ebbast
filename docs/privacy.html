MyDues Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

MyDues helps you keep track of your subscriptions and other recurring payments (household bills, taxes and fees, tuition, insurance), their cost and their due dates. It's built so that your information stays with you: on your devices and in your own private iCloud. MyDues has no user accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics and no advertising.

This policy also appears inside the app under Settings › Privacy. If the two ever differ, this document is the reference.

Information MyDues stores

MyDues saves the following in the app's storage on your iPhone or iPad:

  • the name you enter during onboarding, and the language you choose for the app
  • your monthly budget and currency, and your check-in and reminder preferences
  • the subscriptions and payments you add (name, plan, price, billing cycle, category, color, payment date, reminder choice)
  • your weekly check-in answers (whether you used each subscription in a given week)
  • the purchases, warranties, free trials and pagoPA notices you add

iCloud sync. If you're signed in to iCloud, this information is also saved in your private iCloud database (CloudKit) and in iCloud key-value storage, so it isn't lost if you delete the app or change device, and it stays in sync on your devices signed in with the same Apple Account. Only you can access your private iCloud data; MyDues's developers can't see it. Apple stores it encrypted, and with Advanced Data Protection turned on it's end-to-end encrypted. You can see and delete it in the Settings app under your name › iCloud. If you're not signed in to iCloud, everything stays on the device.

This information isn't sent to us or to anyone else. Like other app data, it's also included in your device's iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on.

Notifications

Check-in, payment, return, warranty, trial and pagoPA reminders are scheduled with local notifications on your device, so no reminder content leaves your device. iCloud sync uses Apple's silent push notifications only to tell the app that your data changed on another device.

Widgets

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show your upcoming payments and monthly total. The app saves a small summary for them in a container on your device that only MyDues and its widgets can read. Anyone who can see your Home Screen or Lock Screen can see what the widgets show.

Apple Intelligence

When MyDues looks for a service's website or picks its plans or cancellation link, it may use Apple Intelligence with the model that runs on your device (Apple's Foundation Models framework). MyDues doesn't use Private Cloud Compute or any server-based model, and nothing about you is sent for this. On devices without Apple Intelligence, these suggestions are simply not offered.

Tracking and third parties

MyDues doesn't track you across apps or websites, doesn't include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and doesn't sell or share data with data brokers. The external services MyDues contacts are Apple's iCloud (for sync, if you're signed in), the public web pages of services you ask it to look up (see below), pagoPA's official site when you choose to pay a notice and, if the developers have configured one, the web address that hosts the updated list of service plans and prices.

Plan suggestions and cheaper alternatives

When you add a subscription by hand, MyDues can suggest the plans of well-known services and their typical prices, and it points out cheaper plans or similar services. This uses a list of plans included in the app. The app may also download an updated copy of that list from a web address chosen by MyDues's developers, at most once a day. That request is a plain download: it contains nothing about you or your subscriptions, and the comparison happens on your device.

Plans and cancellation pages. "See Plans and Prices" and "Cancel Subscription" open the service's own pages, and only when you tap them. For well-known services the links are part of the app's list; for Apple's services, your Apple Account subscriptions open. For any other service, your device finds the service's website (from its name, with a suggestion from Apple Intelligence on the device, always checked by loading the site) and reads its public home and help pages to find the right link; Apple Intelligence picks among the links actually found there. These pages are loaded without cookies or any information about you, and no search engine is used. The page found is remembered with that subscription (and synced through your iCloud). Plans open in an in-app Safari view; cancellation opens in Safari or in your Apple Account, where you're signed in. MyDues doesn't see what you do on those pages.

Children

MyDues isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

  • Delete individual subscriptions in the app: the deletion syncs to iCloud and your other devices.
  • Settings › Reset MyDues deletes everything from this device, from your iCloud and from your other devices.
  • Deleting the app removes what's on that device; data in your iCloud stays until you reset the app or delete it in the Settings app under your name › iCloud.

Because MyDues doesn't hold your data on any server of its own, there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here and in the app, with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes, such as a new optional feature that accesses more of your information, will be explained in the app before they take effect.
